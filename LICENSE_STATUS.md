# License Status

No repository-wide distribution license has been selected in this candidate baseline.

This absence is intentional and is a blocker for public distribution, reuse claims, a third-party contribution program, certification, marketplace publication, and commercial packaging. It must not be interpreted as an open-source license or broad permission.

The decision must address:

- ownership of existing and generated material;
- intended internal, open, source-available, commercial, or standardization mode;
- documentation, schema, code, profile and example licensing separately where needed;
- contributor terms and provenance;
- trademarks and conformance marks;
- third-party references and compatible use;
- warranty, liability, support and export considerations;
- license migration and versioning.

See `docs/next/00_GAEP_Product_Strategy/008_DISTRIBUTION_LICENSE_AND_ECOSYSTEM.md` and `docs/next/06_GAEP_On_GAEP/003_DECISION_REGISTER.md`.
