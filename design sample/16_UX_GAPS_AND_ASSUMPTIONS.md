# UX Gaps and Assumptions

## How to use this register

Items marked High risk should be resolved before the corresponding high-fidelity screen is approved. Provisional treatments make the prototype coherent but are not confirmed business behavior. Do not bury these decisions in generated frames.

| GAP-002 | No approved brand or product name | Visual identity could be mistaken for customer-approved branding | Use provisional `Maritime ERP` + Northstar theme and label as provisional | Medium | What customer/product brand assets and naming must replace the provisional identity? |

## Decisions required before Figma approval

At minimum resolve GAP-001, 005–010, 015, 017–022, 028–030, 035–036, 042–044, and 046. The prototype can be generated before resolution if assumptions remain visibly annotated, but those screens should not be treated as implementation-ready.
