# Client Presentation Checklist

## Narrative and readiness

- [ ] Presenter can explain the platform shell, operational problem, recovery workflow, and planning foundation in 10–12 minutes.
- [ ] Starting frame is Schedule Monitor or a deliberate short Sign In sequence.
- [ ] Primary and fallback flows are both rehearsed.
- [ ] No demo interaction depends on hover alone.
- [ ] The closing frame is the refreshed Schedule Monitor.

## Demonstration data

- [ ] All data is fictional or customer-approved.
- [ ] Service, Voyage Number, CVN, vessel/IMO, ports/UN/LOCODE, and dates remain consistent across frames.
- [ ] Local/UTC pairs are plausible and chronologically consistent.
- [ ] The +11 h deviation and projected changes match detail and timeline views.
- [ ] No cost, cargo, routing, live AIS, or publication claim appears.

## Clickability and recovery

- [ ] Every planned click target is large and visible at presentation zoom.
- [ ] Drawer Close, modal Cancel, and page breadcrumbs are connected.
- [ ] Prebuilt Simulation changed/applied frames are available if interactive components fail.
- [ ] Monitor Table View is available if the Gantt is difficult to present remotely.
- [ ] There are no links to unfinished placeholder modules.

## Claims and terminology

- [ ] The presenter calls the theme and sample data provisional/fictitious where relevant.
- [ ] The standalone-source versus ERP-shell design context is understood internally.
- [ ] Unsupported approvals, automation, metrics, and audit UI are not described as delivered.
- [ ] Maritime terms match the package content guide.
- [ ] Scenario comparison is factual, not presented as AI recommendation.

## Visual and timing quality

- [ ] Shell, spacing, status, and timeline legends remain consistent.
- [ ] Browser/UI chrome does not crop key actions.
- [ ] Notifications and popovers are closed before moving to the next section.
- [ ] Main flow fits 6 minutes; planning segment fits 4 minutes; close fits 1 minute.
- [ ] A 5-minute Q&A fallback route is ready for administration, feeder, distance, or accessibility questions.
