# GAEP Constitution

## Proposed status

This Constitution is Proposed and not approved. It has no current power to authorize implementation, organizational mandate, procurement, production action, public conformance claim, or supersession of the legacy Draft. Its requirements define the candidate durable boundary to review.

If approved, this Constitution would govern official GAEP Core specifications, profiles, organizational bindings, workspaces, realizations, adapters, conformance claims, and official product behavior. Product strategy, implementation convenience, vendor behavior, majority preference, and repository write access would remain lower authority.

## Preamble

Artificial intelligence can accelerate engineering understanding and action. Acceleration can also amplify missing context, unsupported claims, inconsistent decisions, security exposure, unreviewed side effects, and organizational dependence on transient tools or individual memory.

GAEP exists to make human-AI engineering change more accountable, evidence-aware, portable, proportionate, and contestable. It does not transfer human or institutional responsibility to AI, a repository, an implementation, or a conformance label.

## Constitutional interpretation

The uppercase normative terms in the addressable requirements below have the meaning defined by `GAEP-CST-003`. Descriptive article text explains intent but does not create an additional requirement. Where constitutional requirements appear to conflict, the accountable authority must seek an interpretation that preserves human rights and lawful obligation, legitimate authority, safety, evidence integrity, and the narrowest necessary constraint. An unresolved constitutional conflict produces an explicit blocked or exception-review state; it does not create silent permission.

## Article I — Mission and governed scope

GAEP's durable mission is to define portable governance semantics for accountable human-AI engineering. A Product is a durable Managed Asset with identity and history independent of the bounded Initiatives that create, change, operate, migrate, or retire it. Engineering Initiatives may target Product and non-Product Managed Assets. Applicability determines which profiles and activities are relevant; Product-specific practice is not universal law. This is proposed replacement language for separate version-bound constitutional consideration and has no superseding effect while this Constitution remains unapproved.

| Requirement ID | Constitutional requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| GAEP-CST-REQ-001 | GAEP SHALL preserve accountable human or legally recognized organizational responsibility for consequential engineering decisions and effects. | Authority and decision scenario review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-002 | GAEP SHALL distinguish durable governed subjects, including Product Managed Assets, from bounded Initiatives and Changes, and SHALL distinguish Core semantics, profiles, organizational bindings, workspaces, realizations, adapters, guidance, examples, and external systems. | Subject-lifetime, dependency, and conformance review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-003 | GAEP SHALL determine applicable rigor from declared subject, scope, consequence, risk, authority, and policy; availability of a capability SHALL NOT make it universally mandatory. | Applicability scenario review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-004 | GAEP SHALL NOT claim authority, correctness, safety, compliance, readiness, or value merely because content was generated, stored, merged, executed, or labeled conforming. | Negative claim scenario |

## Article II — Human accountability and legitimate authority

Authority is attributable and bounded. Execution capability, authorship, seniority, system access, funding, or AI confidence does not independently establish the right to decide.

| Requirement ID | Constitutional requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| GAEP-CST-REQ-005 | Every consequential decision or authorization SHALL identify the accountable authority, role, subject, scope, exact revision or digest, outcome, conditions, time, validity, and evidence considered. | Decision-record review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-006 | AI, automation, an adapter, or a realization SHALL NOT approve its own material output, accept its own residual risk, or represent itself as the accountable human authority. | Self-approval negative scenario |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-007 | Delegation SHALL identify delegator, delegate, permitted capability, scope, duration, constraints, revocation, and retained accountability. | Delegation scenario review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-008 | Missing, ambiguous, expired, conflicted, or unverifiable authority SHALL resolve explicitly and SHALL NOT be interpreted as permission. | Authority-resolution test |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-009 | A participant SHALL have a defined path to challenge or appeal incorrect authority, evidence, policy application, state, or decision within an appropriate scope. | Contestability scenario |

## Article III — Human dignity, agency, and affected-party protection

Governed evidence can protect accountability and can also become surveillance. GAEP must preserve dignity, lawful rights, meaningful human agency, and proportional treatment of affected people.

| Requirement ID | Constitutional requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| GAEP-CST-REQ-010 | GAEP use SHALL respect applicable human rights, labor, accessibility, privacy, nondiscrimination, consultation, and due-process obligations. | Rights and jurisdiction review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-011 | Data collected for workflow evidence or product learning SHALL NOT be repurposed for individual productivity ranking, disciplinary inference, or model improvement without a separate lawful, transparent, purpose-specific, and approved basis. | Purpose-use audit |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-012 | Affected people SHALL receive understandable notice, recourse, and access appropriate to the consequence of a GAEP-supported decision when law, policy, or ethical review requires it. | Affected-party scenario |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-013 | Human approval SHALL be meaningful: the approver SHALL have sufficient competence, information, time, independence, and available outcomes to exercise judgment. | Approval-quality review |

## Article IV — Claims, evidence, uncertainty, and truthfulness

GAEP separates fluent output from warranted confidence. Evidence is scoped and fallible. Unknown and conflicting information remain visible until resolved or accepted by authority.

| Requirement ID | Constitutional requirement | Verification |

| GAEP-CST-REQ-015 | AI confidence, user confidence, approval, popularity, repetition, and successful execution SHALL NOT be treated as evidence of correctness by themselves. | Evidence-classification scenario |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-016 | Missing, stale, conflicting, unavailable, disconfirming, and lower-authority information SHALL remain visible when it could materially change a decision. | Context-conflict scenario |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-017 | A quality, safety, security, compliance, readiness, or outcome claim SHALL be supported by evidence proportionate to its consequence and declared limitations. | Claim-to-evidence review |
| GAEP-CST-REQ-018 | GAEP SHALL preserve the distinction between observation, evidence, hypothesis, assumption, finding, decision, approval, and unknown. | Semantic conformance review |

## Constitutional conformance review

A candidate GAEP release or action is constitutionally reviewable only when its evidence answers, for the applicable scope:

- Who is accountable and legitimately authorized?
- What exact subject, version, state, profile, and binding apply?
- Which claims are evidence-backed, assumed, unknown, stale, or conflicting?
- Which sources are authoritative, and what was omitted or unavailable?
- What people, rights, data, systems, and external parties are affected?
- What risk, architecture, assurance, recovery, and residual-risk decisions apply?
- What does the AI, realization, or adapter actually have permission and capability to do?
- Is the decision reconstructable and contestable?
- Is governance proportionate and its burden measured?
- Can the organization pause, recover, migrate, or exit?
- Which requirements are unsupported or deviated from?
- Does a separate implementation or effect authorization exist?

An unanswered applicable question is recorded as a gap, unknown, deviation, or blocker. It is not answered by confidence.

## Assumptions under constitutional review

This candidate assumes, but has not yet proved, that:

- a stable vendor-neutral Core can remain both meaningful and usable;
- explicit authority, state, evidence, and trace can improve outcomes without unacceptable burden;
- profiles can preserve Core invariants while fitting diverse initiatives;
- repository-visible governance can coexist with external authoritative systems;
- affected-person protections can be operationalized across organizational and jurisdictional contexts;
- implementation and product pressure will not overwhelm constitutional change control.

These assumptions may justify experiments but not silent weakening of the Proposed requirements.
