# Core Open Decision Register

## Status vocabulary

- `open`: no approved Decision Record closes the question;
- `open-conflicts-with-current-contract`: the question remains open but current normative text already assumes an answer and must be reconciled;
- `deferred`: an approved Decision Record postpones the decision with owner, trigger, and expiry;
- `decided`: an exact approved Decision Record and resulting contract revisions close the question.

The owning-document question is copied exactly. `Closure evidence` states the minimum package, not evidence that closure already occurred. `GAEP-SELF-009` records eight bounded proposed Role Assignments referenced by `GAEP-REG-008`; none is accepted or effective and no current scoped standing Authority Grant exists. All other concrete assignments remain `unassigned`. A proposed assignment, registered role type, author, or repository account does not make a Decision effective.

## Decision criticality and gate classification

Classification does not decide or defer a question. Every row remains governed by its canonical status in the Register. Until an approved Decision Record closes a row or approves its deferral, the listed gate effect applies. A non-blocking deferral must name the excluded scope, owner assignment, trigger, expiry, re-entry test, risk treatment, and prohibited reliance; a tier label alone is never a deferral.

- `T0 — baseline semantics`: the answer changes portable identity, invariant meaning, Core boundary, or the meaning of a baseline claim. The candidate baseline is blocked; ordinary deferral is not acceptable for a baseline that includes the affected contract.
- `T1 — conformance/profile contract`: an approved, bounded deferral may permit a baseline only when the baseline makes no claim that depends on the answer. The affected conformance class, Profile, federation path, or evidence claim remains blocked.
- `T2 — Realization/pilot contract`: an approved scope exclusion may permit the documentation baseline, but the affected pilot and implementation slice remain blocked.
- `T3 — deferred capability`: the first baseline and pilot may exclude the capability through an approved expiring deferral. No marketplace, extension, federation, or ecosystem capability may rely on the unanswered semantics.
