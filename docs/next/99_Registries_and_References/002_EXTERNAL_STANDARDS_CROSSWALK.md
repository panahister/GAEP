## Four-layer use model

| Layer | Examples | GAEP treatment |
|---|---|---|
| GAEP-native constitutional governance | human authority; durable governed state; candidate, review, accept and commit separation | owned by GAEP and never attributed to a source by association |
| External standards and guidance | ISO/IEC/IEEE lifecycle and architecture standards; NIST frameworks; WCAG | version-bound calibration within recorded access and copyright limits |
| Selectable methods and operating models | strategic DDD; EventStorming; Team Topologies; Agile principles | selected and tailored by Initiative applicability, competence, risk and evidence |
| Replaceable tools, providers and adapters | Figma; Codex; Claude Code; GitHub; Jira | may realize or reference governed work but do not define lifecycle phases, method authority or canonical truth |

TOGAF is treated as an enterprise-architecture framework, C4 as a visualization model, DORA as a metric framework, and Figma as an optional Product Design adapter outside this reference catalog. Bounded Context, module, deployable unit, service, repository, team and data boundary remain separate decisions.

## P02 and P03 projection contract

### P02 inputs

P02 may consume catalog reference identity, type, version, current status, official URI, checked date, access evidence, evidence status, claim language, limitations, mappings, and review triggers. It must add competitor or alternative evidence in its own canonical benchmark model and must not reclassify a methodology or tool as a competitor merely to fill a matrix. Executive claims require separate owners, evidence, approval and expiry.

### P03 inputs

P03 may project the concern map, Product-to-Operations rationale, diagrams, reference appendix, and method-versus-tool explanation. It must bind every generated section to catalog ID and version, preserve the source limitations, and keep Figma and other tools out of canonical lifecycle phase names. The Guideline may simplify presentation but cannot create reference truth.

## Change and regeneration

Run `ruby scripts/render_methodology_crosswalk.rb --write` after an intentional catalog change and `ruby scripts/render_methodology_crosswalk.rb --check` during verification. A source edition, official URI, revision state, access basis, mapping, claim, concern, limitation, or review-trigger change requires a catalog revision, regenerated projection, impact review, and applicable human acceptance. Chat output or a manually edited generated row is not a catalog update.
