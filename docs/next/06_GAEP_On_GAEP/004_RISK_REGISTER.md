# GAEP-on-GAEP Risk Register

No approved Risk Profile, likelihood scale, impact scale, tier, assessor, or evidence method exists. Every likelihood, plausibility, impact-severity, control-effectiveness, and residual-risk assessment is therefore `not-assessed`; row order is editorial triage only. The controlled treatment vocabulary comes from `GAEP-CORE-005`: avoid, reduce, transfer/share, monitor, exploit where an opportunity applies, or accept through accountable decision. No residual risk has an approved Risk Acceptance.

Each row is a candidate material Risk Record at revision `0.1.0`, bound to this document revision. The summary table and field-completion tables together form each record. A value of `not-assessed` is an explicit result caused by absent method, authority, or evidence; it is not a hidden score. Every listed control is candidate-only and its effectiveness is `not-assessed`.

Review occurs when product identity, target segment, distribution, Core semantics, data scope, AI autonomy, provider, runtime boundary, threat model, pilot result, or implementation authorization changes.
