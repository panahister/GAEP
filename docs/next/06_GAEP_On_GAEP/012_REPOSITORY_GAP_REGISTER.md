# GAEP Repository Gap Register

## Status and interpretation

This register consolidates gaps found during the pre-implementation repository analysis. It describes the Proposed candidate as reviewed through 2026-07-20; it does not claim approval, conformance, product validation, pilot readiness, baseline readiness, implementation readiness, or implementation authorization.

`Severity` is editorial triage (`blocker`, `high`, or `medium`), not a Core Risk Tier or Risk Assessment. Human assignment choices have been supplied for a bounded subset of roles, but they do not become effective until canonical Role Assignment records are created and explicitly accepted. Roles not covered by those choices remain unassigned; repository authorship, filesystem ownership, and chat identity establish no authority.

Current-status values mean:

- `resolved-by-candidate-structure`: the missing structural home or boundary now exists in the Proposed candidate; evidence, approval, and operational effectiveness may still be absent;
- `open-needs-decision`: one or more Decision Outcomes remain unresolved;
- `open-needs-evidence`: required empirical, review, or assurance evidence is absent;
- `open-needs-assignment`: accountable identity, appointing authority, capacity, or delegation is absent;
- `open-needs-validation`: a candidate contract exists but required integration, scenario, or deterministic validation has not established the claim;
- `open-blocker`: the missing condition prevents the named gate from legitimately passing.

## Closure rule

A gap may move to `resolved-by-candidate-structure` only when the missing structural home or boundary exists and its source is cited. A structural resolution that introduces or exposes a breaking semantic choice must retain a separate open decision or compatibility gap until that choice is legitimately resolved. A gap may close as an evidence, decision, assignment, validation, or authorization gap only through the corresponding Core record and exact gate evidence.
